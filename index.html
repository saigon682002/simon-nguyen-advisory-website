Why work
with Simon.

Four questions worth answering before any engagement starts.

01

Who

I'm Simon, founder and principal advisor of Simon Nguyen Advisory. I spent nearly two decades at J.P. Morgan leading corporate real estate and major capital programmes across international markets, sitting on the client side of every decision. Having been there, I now bring that perspective directly to the business leaders facing those same decisions today.

02

What

I advise owner-occupiers, investors, and the specialist firms who serve them, on end-to-end capital programmes, from business case and strategy through to delivery and leadership. I bring the clarity of someone who has sat inside that world, understanding the governance and risk behind every decision. I add most value early, before resources are committed, whatever the scale of the business.

03

Why

Direct access to me, not a team. That means full focus, and a client-side perspective that a large consultancy can't offer. Having sat on both sides of the table, I understand the decisions a client faces and what it takes to deliver them.

04

What's in it for you

You get a thinking partner who works alongside you and your team, asking questions, challenging assumptions and offering practical advice grounded in experience. I bring independent judgement and constructive challenge, working alongside your existing team rather than replacing it.
